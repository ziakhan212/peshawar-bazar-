Peshawar Bazar - Company/Admin Order Flow

1. Only the admin can create/approve/suspend company accounts.
2. Approved companies use the separate Company Portal to publish products.
3. Customers do not receive company login/contact details and do not communicate directly with companies through the app.
4. A customer submits an order with name, father name, address, phone and payment method.
5. The order is stored on the server as pending_admin and is visible in Admin Panel. If ADMIN_EMAIL is configured, the admin also receives an email.
6. Admin chooses an approved company and forwards the order.
7. Only after admin forwarding does the company see the customer's order/delivery information. If company email/SMTP is configured, the company also receives an email.
8. Company and customer are connected through the Peshawar Bazar admin workflow, not by a direct app relationship.

Environment variables:
ADMIN_USERNAME=admin
ADMIN_PASSWORD=<strong admin password>
ADMIN_EMAIL=<admin email for order notifications, optional>
SMTP_HOST=<smtp host>
SMTP_PORT=587
SMTP_USER=<smtp username>
SMTP_PASS=<smtp password>
SMTP_FROM=<from address, optional>
